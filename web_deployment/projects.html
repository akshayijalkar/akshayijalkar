Research Prototype — In Development

Privacy-Preserving Decentralized Information Retrieval

An experimental decentralized information retrieval system exploring whether nearby participating devices can collaboratively discover and retrieve useful information without depending entirely on conventional Internet infrastructure or a centralized search service.

The initial prototype is being designed around local peer discovery, distributed queries, local document indexing, information retrieval, result aggregation, and ranking.
