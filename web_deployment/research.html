Research Exploration — Topic Not Yet Finalized

Exploring Research Problems Across AI, ML & Computing Systems

Rather than presenting one idea as an established specialization, I am currently reviewing literature, strengthening fundamentals and testing candidate ideas. Areas under exploration include AI/ML systems, intelligent information retrieval, security and privacy, networked systems, cloud/edge computing and resilient computing.

How can AI, machine learning and modern computing systems be combined to solve useful problems with measurable improvements in intelligence, efficiency, security, privacy or resilience?

The initial system concept consists of multiple devices connected through a local network. Each participating node maintains a local index of deliberately shared information. A query originating from one device can be propagated to available peers, processed against their local indexes, and returned to the originating device for aggregation and ranking.

Early prototypes will use public or deliberately shared test documents rather than private browser histories or personal information. Privacy-preserving mechanisms can then be introduced and evaluated as the research develops.

Conceptual Architecture

The initial experimental architecture follows a decentralized query-and-retrieval model:

User Query → Local Peer Discovery → Distributed Search → Local Retrieval → Result Aggregation → Ranking → User

Each participating node is expected to provide a small set of capabilities including peer discovery, local indexing, query processing, result generation, and communication with other participating nodes.

Initial Research Methodology

1. Local Information Collection

Construct controlled collections of public or deliberately shared documents across multiple experimental nodes.

2. Local Indexing

Each node independently creates a searchable representation of its locally available information.

3. Peer Discovery

Investigate mechanisms through which participating devices can discover available peers on a local network.

4. Distributed Query Processing

Propagate information requests to available nodes and perform retrieval against their local indexes.

5. Result Aggregation & Ranking

Combine results returned by participating nodes and investigate methods for ranking information according to relevance.

6. Experimental Evaluation

Evaluate the prototype under different numbers of nodes, information distributions, network conditions, and peer availability scenarios.

Questions I Am Exploring

How can nearby computing devices discover useful information without depending entirely on centralized Internet services?
How should queries be distributed efficiently when the number and availability of participating peers continuously changes?
How can results returned from independent devices be ranked and combined effectively?
What information should a participating device reveal to other peers, and what information should remain private?
How does decentralized retrieval compare with centralized information retrieval in latency, network overhead, availability, and retrieval quality?
Can artificial intelligence improve peer selection, information ranking, query routing, or retrieval quality in decentralized information systems?
