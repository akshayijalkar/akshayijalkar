Research & Academic Profile

Welcome to my academic and research portfolio. I am transitioning into Computer Science with a focus on computer networks , distributed systems , cybersecurity , cloud technologies , and decentralized information systems .

My long-term goal is to pursue research in Computer Science, particularly in developing secure systems and resilient systems that enable information access and communication even in environments with limited or no Internet connectivity.

Current Research Idea

I am exploring a local-area information discovery system that can retrieve useful information from nearby participating devices without relying entirely on Internet connectivity.

The system would allow devices in a local network to share selected, privacy-controlled information such as cached resources, bookmarks, documents, or indexed knowledge.
